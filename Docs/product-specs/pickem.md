# Product Spec: Pick'em

**Author**: Derek *(written up by Claude from Derek's request on 2026-10-08; review before approving)*
**Status**: approved
**Last updated**: 2026-10-08

---

## Problem

Markets reward trading well. Many fans just want to call the season once, the way people fill
out a March Madness bracket, and then compare with friends as it plays out.

## Goal

Before each future ASL season, anyone can pick players through every stage down to a champion,
see everyone else's bracket, see who was most accurate, and earn achievements for doing well.

Derek's words: "a pick em style option for future asl seasons where anyone can select players
through each stage of ASL to win eventually a winner of the whole thing. And then you can
publicly see other players brackets, the players can also be sorted by who was the most
accurate. It's similar in concept to march madness. Players can earn achievements for how well
they do in these pickems each season."

## Non-Goals

- Prizes of any value, including minerals (play money rule; see Open Questions).
- Pick'ems for seasons already played.
- Picking individual matches or map scores.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] A signed in fan can fill out and save a full bracket before the season starts.
- [ ] After the season starts, every bracket is public.
- [ ] Standings can be sorted by accuracy as well as points.
- [ ] Each season awards achievements based on how a bracket did.

## Open Questions

- [x] Should a pick'em pay minerals to the top brackets? No; achievements only — Derek, 2026-10-08
- [x] Are the points (1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 per round) right? Yes — Derek, 2026-10-08
- [x] Hide brackets until the lock, or show them from the start? Hide until the lock — Derek, 2026-10-08
- [x] A career page of each person's achievements across seasons? Not for now; per season only — Derek, 2026-10-08
